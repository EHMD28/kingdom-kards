# Kingdom Kards Rules

## Overview

Kingdom Kards can be played with 2 - 5 players. At the start of the game, each player has 100 points. The objective of the game is to lower your opponents' points to 0. Once a player's points reach 0, they are eliminated from the game. 

There are 3 locations cards can be in during the game: hand, deck, or discard pile. You are the only one allowed to look at the cards in your hand and discard pile. The deck is where the cards you haven't drawn yet are. The discard pile is where cards go after either being played, or being discarded. 


## Setup

Each player has a standard, 52-card deck. At the start of the game, each player draws 5 cards from the top of their deck. The turn player is chosen randomly. If you're playing in person, use a dice, or a random number picker. 

## Turns

At the start of each player's turn, they draw 1 card from their deck. If a player tries to draw from their deck, but they have no cards in their deck, they shuffle the cards in their discard pile and it becomes their new deck. If a player cannot shuffle cards into their discard pile, they are eliminated (but this shouldn't ever happen during normal gameplay).

During their turn, a player can make any number of [actions](##actions). Once a player is done performing actions, they end their turn.

## Actions

There are several actions a player can take during their turn. When a card is played, it is moved from the hand to the top of the player's discard pile, alongside any attachments (talked about later). 

#### King

When a player plays a King, they can either play the King by itself or with a single number card, which is called an attachment. The King decreases a chosen player's points by 10 + the number card attached. If a King is played by itself, it will deal 10 damage. If a King is played with a 5, it will deal 15 damage.

#### Queen

When a player plays a Queen, can attach a single number card to it. They gain points equal to the 10 \+ the attachment.

#### Jack

When a player is targeted by a King, they can play a Jack. This makes any damage they would take from the King 0.

#### Ace

The turn player can add one card from their discard pile to their hand, except another Ace.

#### Number

The turn player discards _n_ cards, then draws _n_ cards where _n_ is the value of the number.
